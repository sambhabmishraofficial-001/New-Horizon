Discover how a full Virtual Research Institute explores data, runs virtual laboratories, and carries every program from question to discovery

Data

Your institute holds literature, datasets, instruments, and experimental results in one research fabric. Every claim stays linked to its source so AI scientists and human investigators can retrieve, compare, and extend what the institute already knows—then return new findings to the shared record.

Workflows

Operate complete research programs across a network of virtual laboratories: frame the question, survey prior art, generate hypotheses, design experiments, run analyses, and synthesize results. The institute coordinates each stage so teams move from open problem to defensible next experiment without rebuilding the process each time.

Notebooks

Institute notebooks keep analysis, models, and scientific judgment tied to the underlying evidence. Start from laboratory templates or bring your own methods, collaborate across virtual labs, and leave a reproducible trail so every discovery remains reviewable long after the original team moves on.

Memory

The institute remembers how science actually progressed—what was attempted, what failed, why a path was chosen, and which investigator still holds the context. When new work begins, the Virtual Research Institute surfaces the relevant prior run, the expert who solved it before, and the next action the program should take.

Experiments

Virtual laboratories capture discarded runs, unused measurements, and unpublished observations as first-class institute assets. AI scientists query that experimental history, recognize when a new protocol echoes an old dead end, and propose stronger conditions so discovery compounds across the entire research institute.
